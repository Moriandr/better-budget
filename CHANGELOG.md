# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- First public release.
- Plain-English transaction entry form; no accounting knowledge needed.
- Generic Chart of Accounts with dropdowns and a Settings table.
- Monthly reports with a corrected cash flow statement (checked against actual cash movement).
- Dashboard driven by the Dashboard Group column and the currency setting.
- Weekly budget email with on/off menu items.
- First-run set-up creates the starter chart, first ledger and a Start Here tab.
